My name is Tonmoy Saha. My research focuses on Non-Destructive Evaluation (NDE) using ultrasonic waveforms and physics-based machine learning for autonomous, real-time monitoring in advanced manufacturing.

At Clarkson University’s Photo-Acoustic Research (PAR) Lab, I develop robotics-enabled sensing systems and validate physics-guided ML models that solve challenging inverse problems, converting ultrasonic measurements into reliable indicators of material state and process quality. I’m especially focused on deploying ML/DL models into robotic workflows for automated sensing, inspection, and process control using ROS.

I’m highly skilled in finite element analysis using Abaqus and ANSYS, and I integrate instrumentation and robotics with tools such as LabVIEW, MATLAB, Mathematica and Python to enable end-to-end experimental and simulation-driven workflows across diverse manufacturing environments.

I enjoy learning new technologies, thrive under pressure, and adapt quickly to challenging situations.
